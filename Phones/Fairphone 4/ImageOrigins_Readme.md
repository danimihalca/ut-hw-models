What was changed ? : 
- image was AI upscaled with upscayl 
- phone edges were refined or remade
- image shadows highlights were changed
- image was sharpened + contrast
- cleaned up image and transparent background
- screen edge were refined or remade
- Ubuntu touch taskbar, wallpaper were aded
- camera lenses,flash could be slightly different from original
